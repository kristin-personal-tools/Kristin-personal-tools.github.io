School Executive Assistant — Privacy

This page concerns only the School Executive Assistant, not the separate personal Calendar application.

Purpose and information accessed

The application assists its owner with academic correspondence, deadlines and planning. With authorization, it retrieves school Gmail headers, subjects, message bodies and thread context. Its only Google permission is gmail.readonly; it does not send, delete or modify email. The current routine collector does not download attachments.

Processing and storage

Information is processed on a private virtual server. Relevant email content may also be processed by the AI provider selected for the assistant. Local review files, assistant conversation history and planning records may retain email-derived information. Processing is not confined to Gmail or to the owner’s personal computer.

Sensitive personal or pastoral material is to be flagged for the owner rather than routinely summarized into planning records. The ability to read messages can nevertheless expose such information during review.

Retention and control

Local records are not automatically deleted after each review. There is currently no fixed automatic deletion schedule for these records. Revoking Google access prevents future authorized retrieval but does not delete previously retained copies. Retention by an AI provider is governed by the terms and settings of the service in use.

The owner can revoke the application’s access through her Google account. Revocation and removal of retained records are separate actions.

Use and disclosure

The intended use is private academic assistance, not advertising or sale of email information. The application involves server and AI service providers as described above. Its operation must comply with school policies and applicable Google API requirements. This page does not claim school endorsement or Google verification.
