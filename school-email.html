School Executive Assistant

A private application for Kristin to review her school email for academic correspondence, deadlines and follow-up work. This is a personal tool, not an official school service or an application offered to the public.

Read-only access

The application requests only Google’s Gmail read-only permission (gmail.readonly). It can read email messages and metadata, but cannot send or delete messages, mark them read or change labels. The current routine collector does not download attachments.

How information is handled

Email is processed on a private virtual server by an AI assistant. Relevant content may be sent to the AI provider used by the assistant. Email-derived information may be retained in review files, conversation history and academic planning records. Use must comply with applicable school policies.
