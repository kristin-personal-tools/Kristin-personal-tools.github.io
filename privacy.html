Privacy Policy

Hermes – Personal Calendar

Purpose and users

This is a personal-use integration connecting its owner's Google Calendar to a privately operated instance of Hermes Agent. It is not a public sign-up service.

Information accessed and used

With the owner's authorization, the integration accesses Google Calendar event information, which may include titles, dates, times, locations, descriptions and attendees. This information is used to answer the owner's questions, assist with scheduling and manage events at the owner's direction. The integration requests Calendar event permissions, not Gmail access.

Processing and storage

Hermes Agent runs on a privately operated virtual server. Relevant calendar information may be sent to the configured AI service provider to process the owner's requests. Processing is not limited to the owner's personal device.

Conversations, planning records and operational logs may retain calendar information. Authorization credentials are stored separately from this public website. Calendar information and credentials are not intentionally published on this website. Infrastructure and AI service providers' applicable terms and privacy policies also govern their processing and retention.

Control and deletion

The owner can revoke the integration's access through their Google Account's third-party connections settings. Revocation stops future authorized access, but does not automatically erase information already retained in conversations, planning records, logs or provider systems. Those records must be managed separately using the relevant system or provider controls.

This website

This website provides information about the integration. It does not display calendar records, offer public registration or include operator-added analytics. It is intended for hosting on GitHub Pages, whose hosting service may process visitor information under GitHub's privacy statement.
