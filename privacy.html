Privacy Policy

Hermes – Personal Calendar

Purpose and access

This is a private, personal-use integration with Hermes Agent. Google account access requires the owner's authorization. Calendar event access supports scheduling and owner-directed event management. Optional Gmail read-only access supports finding and summarizing messages and relevant attachments. Gmail access does not permit sending or changing email.

Processing and storage

Authorized information is processed by the privately operated service and may be sent to its configured AI provider to carry out the owner's requests. Relevant information may remain in conversations, working records and operational logs. Service providers' terms and privacy policies also apply. Account credentials are stored separately from this website.

Control

The owner can revoke access through Google Account third-party connection settings. Revocation stops future authorized access; previously retained records must be managed separately through the relevant system or provider.

This website

This informational website publishes no private calendar records, email messages or account credentials and includes no operator-added analytics. GitHub Pages may process visitor information under GitHub's privacy statement.
